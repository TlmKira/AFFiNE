# AI Dynamic Wallpaper Prompts

These wallpapers are generated for AFFiNE's frosted dynamic background. They
are intentionally low-detail, wide, and text-free because the app applies blur,
desaturation, and theme overlays before showing them behind the UI.

## Shared Negative Prompt

Do not include readable text, title, logo, watermark, gibberish text, dense
small typography, ordinary anime poster composition, light novel cover feeling,
photorealistic humans, cluttered symbols, or high-contrast busy backgrounds.
